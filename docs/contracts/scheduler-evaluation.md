## Scheduler conditions
Three named modes only: batch2 (two-family batch, two outstanding families per owner), batch1 (one-family batch, one outstanding family), credit1 (one-family batch, at most two outstanding families per owner). One active compute job per owner; queued accepted packets consume credit until their contiguous-prefix native submission is acknowledged. A producer's active job is cleared when its validated packet returns, independently of old acknowledgements. Old acknowledgements must never clear a newer active job.

At most two actual WASM owners, each256MiB, total512MiB. credit1 permits at most two outstanding families per owner, including at most one active calculation; both may have returned while waiting for acknowledgement. Each dispatched packet reserves its worst-case bytes until acknowledgement: batch2/batch1 reserve16MiB, credit1 reserves8MiB per single-family packet. Both Worker and host reject larger packets before transfer/acceptance. Total reserved transport, including active and queued packets, is at most32MiB. At most workers*2 outstanding families,16MiB packets and32MiB in-flight native transport. Pending and submitted IDs are unique, within the native finite family budget, with no gaps or repeated dispatch. Only acknowledged contiguous prefixes contribute usable, raw, full/SLP/DLP/cycle/graph counters. Duplicate A handling remains native. All modes use the same unchanged reviewed DLP WASM and baseline geometry: B3250000,halfwidth524288,LP145B,DLP1035B²,offset-23,2048 patterns. No mathematical kernel or threshold change in this stage.

Each audit request optionally captures at most8MiB total packet bytes for post-retirement identity verification; this finite JS audit retention is separate from32MiB transport and WASM memory. Capture is disabled for ordinary use. SHA-256 per native family is computed in the harness from actual returned bytes after all Workers retire; canonical accepted packet framing and family ranges are checked before submission. Fixed-range trials and all common performance prefixes must match byte hashes, polynomial/full/SLP/DLP/cycle/usable/graph counts. Hashes are evidence, not substitutes for native relation/factor verification.

## Metrics and stopping
Keep bounded256-event traces and per-owner cumulative state times. During collection classify host-observed time into assigned-job wall time, coordinator submission, idle blocked by exhausted credit, and other idle. Their sum must equal the collection window. Assigned-job utilization includes native computation/wrappers/delivery and is not CPU utilization. Delivered native sieve time is a lower bound when termination interrupts a call; report the censored active job separately. Packet return-to-ack latency may overlap useful ahead computation, so distinguish it from blocked idle time. Preserve raw invocation/host/Chrome/source/artifact hashes, issued/submitted families, polys/raw/full/SLP/DLP/cycles/usable, graph, requested heap, grown linear memory and sampled Chrome RSS/PSS.

Initialization, computation, delivery and exact host result proof stay inside the original finite request deadline, with init10s, AbortSignal and forced retirement. Source-release/terminate errors fail closed. Factor product/proper divisor and probable-prime status remain validated. Initial assets are followed by server shutdown, offline/cache-disabled CDP audit and zero page/Worker calculation communication; positive detectors and source/owner release remain mandatory.

Stop at the fixed family bound without premature extraction; require all six full-prefix packet hashes and counters identical. Existing40/64-digit full factor controls, malformed inputs/packets, cancellation, deadlines and stale/duplicate/ack/credit races must pass before measurement. These controls and build/test/review time are reported separately.

Then exactly12 fresh-Chrome RSA-10060s trials, two symmetric repeats per mode/owner count in the frozen plan. No retries or overwrites; harness failure preserves evidence and stops. A candidate qualifies only if both two-owner usable yields are at least1.10 times paired batch2 baselines, both one-owner yields at least0.90, all mandatory contracts and equal-family evidence pass, and two-owner blocked-credit-idle sum is no more than baseline+100ms in each repeat. A zero/abnormal baseline fails closed. Rank by the minimum two-owner yield ratio using exact integer cross-products, tie batch1 before credit1. No600s scheduler trials; no completion-time extrapolation.
